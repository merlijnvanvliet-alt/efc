The venue

A bird's eye view
over Amsterdam

Looking out over a capital of nearly a million people, the case makes itself: liveable cities, connectivity that matters economically and socially, and the role electric aviation plays in both.

Amsterdam in Motion was created for the city's 750th anniversary, initiated by Duncan Stutterheim, curated by the Amsterdam Museum and funded by donors including Schiphol Group.
